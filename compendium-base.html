On the Nature of the Anord

"The world does not bleed blood when it is wounded; it bleeds architecture, mathematics, and raw potential. We call this bleeding the Anord, and it is structural failure made manifest."
— High Scriptor Alistair, Year 142

The Anord is not a simple corruption or biological blight, but a systemic unraveling of geometric reality itself. Where the Loom of Creation frays, magic bleeds raw into the soil without the stabilizing architecture of ancient ancestral glyphs or natural ley-lines.

Anatomical Behavior of a Pulse

When a pulse occurs, physical geography shifts overnight. Rivers twist into impossible angles, long-dead structural ruins re-emerge seamlessly inside living root systems, and distance itself becomes elastic. A path that required three days of march may shorten to a hundred paces, or balloon into an impassable void.

Localized Spell Disruptions

Because the mathematical laws governing standard arcana are shattered within an active pulse zone, traditional spell casting becomes highly volatile. Scribes and spell-weavers are strictly ordered to monitor localized containment signatures before executing incantations.

Historical Displacements

Fragments of historical eras long thought consumed by time have been documented reappearing within the deep timber sectors, completely intact but completely hostile to current timeline occupants.
